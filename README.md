## Engineering ecosystem

The current work is intentionally split by responsibility rather than presented as one oversized repository:

```text
Career Intelligence
      ↓
Engineering Handbook
      ↓
Engineering Labs
      ↓
Product / OSS work
      ↓
Verified evidence and iteration
```

- **Career Intelligence** — private canonical career/data system with provenance, reconciliation, confidence and public-safe projections.
- **Engineering Handbook** — engineering theory, architecture, trade-offs and prerequisites.
- **Engineering Labs** — hands-on missions that turn theory into practical engineering evidence.
- **Ladvero / ListingKit** — real product engineering and end-to-end product ownership.
- **Haversine Geolocation** — public OSS history, maintenance, package/release engineering and geospatial work.
- **GitHub governance** — public repository standards, safety boundaries, CI/governance and controlled automation.

The private repositories stay private until their own product, history, security and publication gates are satisfied. The public profile is a projection of verified work, not a mirror of private source material.

## How I engineer

```text
problem
  ↓
constraints & evidence
  ↓
architecture / trade-offs
  ↓
implementation
  ↓
tests + security + observability
  ↓
review
  ↓
release
  ↓
verified outcome
  ↓
next iteration
```

- **Explicit trade-offs over accidental complexity.**
- **Tests, CI, security, and observability are engineering work — not decoration.**
- **AI accelerates engineering; it does not replace verification or ownership.**
- **Historical work should stay honest about its age and context.**
- **Real systems, releases, and contributions matter more than artificial activity.**

<details>
<summary><strong>⚙️ What “AI-assisted engineering” means to me</strong></summary>

<br />

I use AI across research, implementation, debugging, review, documentation, and automation — but I want important decisions to remain inspectable.

The useful pattern is not “AI writes everything.” It is:

```text
intent → agent/tooling → implementation → deterministic checks → review → human-controlled gate
```

That makes AI a force multiplier while preserving engineering responsibility.

</details>
